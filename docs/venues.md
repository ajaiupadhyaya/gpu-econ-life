## Financial Analysts Journal (CFA Institute, published with Taylor & Francis)

- **Scope fit.** CFA Institute describes it as its flagship practitioner journal for investment
  management research. The paper's rent-to-value and earnings-restatement angle fits a
  practitioner audience. Source: https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/submit-to-financial-analysts-journal
- **Length.** Not confirmed. The CFA submit page states no limit; the "Top Tips" PDF only asks
  authors to "have the word count of your piece to hand". The Taylor & Francis instructions
  page (https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=ufaj20)
  returned HTTP 403.
- **Prior SSRN/arXiv posting.** FAJ-specific rule not confirmed. Taylor & Francis's general
  policy lets authors share the Author's Original Manuscript anywhere, including preprint
  servers, and says this "is not considered to be duplicate publication". Source:
  https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/research-impact/sharing-versions-of-journal-articles/
- **Submission fee.** The submission flow includes a billing address, a checkout and a card
  payment step, so a fee is charged at submission. The amount is not confirmed. Source:
  https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/support/faj-top-tips-for-a-smooth-submission.pdf
- **Data/code policy.** Not confirmed.
- **Review.** Anonymised manuscripts go to peer reviewers. The CFA page states "64 average days
  from submission to post-review decision". Source: the CFA submit page above.

## Accounting Horizons (American Accounting Association)

- **Scope fit.** Publishes research and perspectives for academics and practitioners, with
  financial reporting, data analytics and regulation among listed topics. The restatement of
  hyperscaler depreciation fits better here than the pricing econometrics. Source:
  https://aaahq.org/Research/Journals/Accounting-Horizons/Guide-for-Authors
- **Length.** Initial submissions "should be within 25 pages of text (double-spaced, 12-point
  font, and normal margins) and 40 pages in total", excluding title and abstract pages;
  accepted papers are generally within 20 pages of text and 35 in total. Source: editorial
  policy PDF, headed "Updated December 2025", file name dated 04-2026:
  https://aaahq.org/portals/0/documents/journals/Editorial-Policies/2026/Accounting%20Horizons_Editorial%20Policy%2004-2026.pdf
- **Prior SSRN posting.** Allowed. The Guide for Authors says a version on "a working paper
  series such as SSRN does not constitute prior publication". Source: Guide for Authors above.
- **Submission fee.** Non-refundable. The Guide for Authors lists USD 90 for AAA members and
  USD 115 for non-members. A search-engine summary quoted older figures (USD 80/105 and
  75/100), so pay whatever the AAA product catalogue shows on the day
  (https://my.aaahq.org/Shop/Product-Catalog). Late revisions count as new submissions and
  need a new fee.
- **Data/code policy.** At conditional acceptance authors must give the editors a Data
  Description Disclosures list and "submit all computer code used to produce the results",
  and keep final datasets for at least 7 years. This starts on 1 July 2026 for The Accounting
  Review and on 1 July 2027 for the other AAA journals, including this one. Public posting of
  data is encouraged, not required. Source: Guide for Authors above.
- **Review time.** No figure stated; the guide mentions an expedited review for especially
  timely topics.

## Economics Letters (Elsevier)

- **Scope fit.** A letters journal for short communications such as early results; comments
  and teaching notes are not accepted. It suits a spin-off note (for example, the
  contract-type age slopes alone), not the full paper. Source:
  https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/economics-letters/0165-1765
- **Length.** "usually limited to 2,000 words" excluding references. Source: same page.
- **Prior SSRN/arXiv posting.** Elsevier's general policy: "Authors can share their preprint
  anywhere at any time". It notes that some society-owned and double-anonymized journals
  differ, so check the Guide for Authors. Source:
  https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/sharing
- **Submission fee.** Not confirmed on the live page. The Guide for Authors
  (https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/economics-letters/publish/guide-for-authors)
  returned HTTP 403. A search-engine summary of that page reported a non-refundable USD 125
  fee (USD 67.50 for PhD-student contact authors) with waivers for some low-income countries;
  older third-party listings show EUR 50 / USD 65. Elsevier's general submission-fee page
  (https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/submission-fees) did not
  list the journal's amount.
- **Data/code policy.** Not confirmed (Guide for Authors unreadable).
- **Review.** Described as a "fast review process" in which papers are accepted as is or
  rejected; no time figure. Source: the Elsevier shop page above.

## Issues in Political Economy (undergraduate journal; Elon University and University of Mary Washington)

- **Why this one.** A long-running, student-edited, double-blind refereed undergraduate
  economics journal tied to the annual EEA conference. The Undergraduate
  Economic Review (Illinois Wesleyan) was considered but its latest issue is Volume 18 (2021)
  and it is not taking submissions until fall 2026 (https://digitalcommons.iwu.edu/uer/).
- **Scope fit.** Accepts "quality undergraduate research in all areas of economics"; papers
  must be about economics, written during undergraduate study and submitted through a faculty
  sponsor. Source: https://www.elon.edu/u/academics/business/ipe/about-the-journal/
- **Length.** Not stated.
- **Prior posting.** Not stated on either page; not confirmed. Ask the editor before posting
  to SSRN if this venue is chosen.
- **Fee.** None mentioned.
- **Data/code policy.** None stated. A completed AI Use Disclosure Form is required, or the
  submission is not considered.
- **Review and deadlines.** Two student referees per paper, then an editorial board decision.
  Referees are assigned in early February, reports are due in mid-February and decision
  letters go out the week after the board meets. The regular deadline for Volume 36 (Summer
  2027) is 30 January 2027 (17 March 2027 for IPE-EEA conference presenters). Source:
  https://www.elon.edu/u/academics/business/ipe/about-the-journal/journal-submissions/

## arXiv (econ.GN, q-fin.GN)

- **Categories.** econ.GN, General Economics: "General methodological, applied, and empirical
  contributions to economics." q-fin.GN, General Finance: "Development of general quantitative
  methodologies with applications in finance". Source: https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy
- **Endorsement.** A first submission to a category needs at least one endorsement for that
  endorsement domain. An institutional email address speeds this up, and an account can be
  endorsed automatically if it has an institutional email and has claimed a co-authored paper
  in the domain. Otherwise ask an established arXiv author in the field you know personally;
  arXiv warns against mass-emailing endorsers. Endorsers need a set number of papers in the
  domain submitted between three months and five years ago. The page does not say anything
  specific about econ or q-fin. Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html
- **Moderation.** Not peer review, but submissions must be scholarly and substantive and can
  be reclassified to the most relevant category; each author may submit up to two new papers
  a month. Source: https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html

## SSRN

- **What is needed.** A free SSRN user account with a complete author profile; English title,
  abstract and full-text PDF showing the title and all authors with affiliations; the date the
  paper was written; names, affiliations and emails for all authors; an AI disclosure
  statement on the abstract and PDF if AI was used. Source ("What are SSRN's submission
  guidelines?"): https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/get-started
- **Fee.** The page says the account is free; it does not state a posting fee (not confirmed).
- **Screening.** "Most submissions are processed quickly, though some may take up to 10
  business days." The checks cover completeness, formatting, author identity, competing
  interests and permissions. Page last updated 20 July 2026. Source:
  https://www.elsevier.support/ssrn/answer/ssrn-review-process
- **Fit with journals.** Accounting Horizons explicitly allows a prior SSRN version; Taylor &
  Francis (FAJ) and Elsevier (Economics Letters) allow preprints under their general policies.
  JFDS and IPE are not confirmed.
