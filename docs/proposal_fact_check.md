# Proposal fact-check (checked 2026-10-31)

**Verdict: of 18 claims checked, 10 match a primary source, 2 differ, and 6 remain unverified at the primary level.**

Scope: the Motivation section, the Step 5 "17 changes" line, H4, and the Phase 7 prompt in `docs/proposal.md`. Filings were read on sec.gov (document pages and the XBRL "R" note pages). Quotes are kept under 15 words, so some are fragments of longer sentences.

## Access notes

- `www.sec.gov/Archives/...` returned HTTP 403 to a plain `curl` with the neutral User-Agent. `data.sec.gov` (submissions API) returned 200 and was used to list filings, form types and tickers. Filing text was read with the WebFetch tool instead.
- The WebFetch tool reads long 10-K pages in 100,000-character windows and often misses the notes. The XBRL R pages (for example `.../R10.htm`) held the note text, so most citations point to an R page inside the named filing.
- X posts (Burry) and Bloomberg Terminal data are paywalled or blocked. I did not try to get around that.
- Burry's Substack posts are paywalled after the opening, so only the free portions were readable.

## Mismatches that matter for the paper

1. Meta never moved to 6 years, so the "5–6 years" sentence in the Motivation section is wrong for Meta.
2. The A100 2029 contract is CoreWeave's, not Nvidia's.
3. The CoreWeave 95%, the Burry "2–3 years", and the Nebius $167.6M figures have no primary support yet.
4. The Compute Desk article is by Compute Desk's founder, and its H100 forward prices are Compute Desk's own series.
5. Amazon 2025: $1.4B is depreciation and excludes the $920M early-retirement charge booked in Q4 2024.
